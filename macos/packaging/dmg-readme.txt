BHTerminal — by BiswasHost
==========================

A fast, native SSH · SFTP · VNC client for macOS — a free MobaXterm / Termius
alternative. Saved-session tree, real-ssh terminals (keys, agent, passphrase,
2FA, ProxyJump) with tabs & split panes, a side-by-side SFTP browser that
shares the terminal's connection, port forwarding, and secrets kept in the
macOS Keychain. 100% free & open-source.


INSTALL
-------

1) Drag the BHTerminal icon onto the Applications folder (shown here).

2) FIRST LAUNCH — allowing the app:
   BHTerminal is signed but not notarized by Apple (notarization needs a paid
   Apple account), so macOS will say it "could not verify BHTerminal is free
   of malware." This is normal. Approve it once:

     a. Open BHTerminal from your Applications folder. macOS blocks it — click
        "Done".
     b. Open  System Settings  ›  Privacy & Security.
     c. Scroll down to  "BHTerminal was blocked…"  and click  "Open Anyway".
     d. Confirm with Touch ID or your password.

   BHTerminal opens and will not ask again.

3) The first time it reads a saved host password, macOS asks for your login
   keychain password (your Mac account password). Enter it and click
   "Always Allow" — you'll only see this once.
