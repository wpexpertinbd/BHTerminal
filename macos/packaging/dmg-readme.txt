BHTerminal — Installation
==========================

1) Drag the BHTerminal icon onto the Applications folder (shown here).

2) FIRST LAUNCH — allowing the app:
   BHTerminal is signed but not notarized by Apple, so macOS will say it
   "could not verify BHTerminal is free of malware." This is normal. Approve
   it once:

     a. Open BHTerminal from your Applications folder. macOS blocks it — click
        "Done".
     b. Open  System Settings  ›  Privacy & Security.
     c. Scroll down to  "BHTerminal was blocked…"  and click  "Open Anyway".
     d. Confirm with Touch ID or your password.

   BHTerminal opens and will not ask again.

3) The first time it reads a saved host password, macOS asks for your login
   keychain password (your Mac account password). Enter it and click
   "Always Allow" — you'll only see this once.

Enjoy!  — BiswasHost   https://biswashost.com
