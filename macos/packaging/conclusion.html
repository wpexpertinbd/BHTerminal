BHTerminal is installed 🎉

Find BHTerminal in your Applications folder or Launchpad. Add a host, double-click it, and your terminal & SFTP file browser connect together — MobaXterm-style.

First launch: macOS may say the app is from an “unidentified developer” (it isn't notarized — that needs a paid Apple account). Right-click the app → Open, or go to System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Open Anyway. You only do this once. The first time it reads a saved password it asks for your login keychain — click Always Allow.

Free & open-source: github.com/wpexpertinbd/BHTerminal — thank you for using BHTerminal!
